# I. The Interview Process

* The interviewer is looking to evaluate the someone's ability to solve algorithmic questions in a time frame
* The assessmet of the performance is based on:
    * Analytic Skills: How much time the candidate required to solve the problem? How optimal was the solution? Was the structure of the problem well?
    * Coding skills: Was the candidate able to sucesssfully translate algorithms to reasonable code? Was it clean and organized?
    * Technical Knowledge: Do the candidate have a strong foundation in computer science and the relevante technologies?
    * Experience: Have the candidate made good technical decisions in the past? Has he built interesting, challenging projects?

* Whiteboards tend to encourage candidates to speak more and explain their thought process
