# III. Special Situtations

## Experienced Candidates

* More experienced engineers might see slightly less focus on algorithm questions
* Some interviewwers might hold experience candidates to a somewhat lower standard
* Some interviewers hold experience candidates to a higher standars, the more years of experience allow a candidate to have seen many more types of problems

## Startups

* Many startups might post job listings, but for the other startups, often the best way in through a personal referral
* Startups tend to want engineers who are not only smart and who can code, but also people who would work well in an entrepreneurial environment
